not files the skill requires after installation. No source archive or original

timestamp is not a document's publication/edition date or evidence that a historical
announcement describes today's implementation.

- **03** is a crawling/indexing topic directory, not internal parsing/index-partition
  documentation.

  guidance for present implementation claims. In particular, standalone helpful

  require their linked specifications.

  not a complete network/DNS-error reference.
- **33-35** describe metric definitions and measurement, not exhaustive optimization
  recipes despite some local titles.
- **39** is a Product overview linking feature-specific requirements.
- **40**: the archived canonical destination is
  https://developers.google.com/search/updates, **not an FAQ/Q&A implementation
  guide**. The history records FAQ rich-result retirement from May 7, 2026 and
  removal of its documentation in June. Do not reuse obsolete eligibility advice.
- **41** is BreadcrumbList guidance only; Organization requires a separate source.
- **42** is title-link guidance only; snippet advice also comes from 05 and the
  separately linked official snippet documentation.
- **49 and 50** are broad/overview guidance, not a substitute for the full
  localized-version, facet, pagination, or offer specifications.
- **53** is About Search Console, not the complete indexing-report specification.
- **54-56** are dossiers **generated by `scripts\download_all.mjs`**, not downloaded
  primary leak documents, trial exhibits, or a complete patent. Treat their
  unreferenced production-mechanism claims as unverified secondary assertions.
- The current local site-reputation policy includes an August 2026 EEA distinction;
  do not treat its 2024 announcement as the current global enforcement procedure.

## Evidence classes

**Official guidance** means the supplied body is an official Google help/technical
document. **Official history** means a dated Google explanation or changelog.
**Generated secondary** means a locally generated dossier, not its underlying
primary source. These classes describe provenance, not guaranteed currentness.
