# Content quality, E-E-A-T, and reviews

Source entries: **10-18, 52**. The local quality-rater PDF is the
**September 11, 2025 edition, 182 pages**, not a 170-page current manual.
Page references below use that edition.

## Quality is purpose-relative

Identify the page's beneficial purpose and the main content that achieves it.
Main content can be text, media, a working tool, a product specification panel,
or a useful discussion. Supplementary content supports the experience; ads and
monetization are not automatically low quality but must not mislead or obstruct.

Assess:

| Dimension | Evidence to look for |
| --- | --- |
| Purpose/task completion | The page delivers the answer, tool, product, or experience it promises |
| Effort | Real research, curation, testing, reporting, or useful systems |
| Originality | New evidence, insight, explanation, or functionality beyond sources |
| Skill | Clear communication, production quality, usable functionality |
| Accuracy | Checkable facts, up-to-date material details, appropriate consensus |
| Completeness | Enough information for the task without filler or evasive answers |
| Trust | Responsible creator/site, transparent incentives, consistent claims |

Credit and citations alone do not replace original effort. Automated content can
be valuable, but mass-generated pages with no meaningful original value remain
problematic. Do not mistake AI use itself for proof of spam.

## E-E-A-T

Experience, expertise, authoritativeness, and trustworthiness are a qualitative
framework. Trust is central, and the required kind of experience/expertise depends
on the page's purpose. Not every helpful page must demonstrate every aspect.

Look for accurate authorship, appropriate bylines, real biographies, responsibility
for publication, relevant credentials where needed, first-hand evidence, independent
reputation, accessible contact/support, and transparent editorial practices.

Do not invent credentials, author identities, expert reviews, hands-on experiences,
awards, customer testimonials, or citations. A biography widget or schema object
cannot turn weak content into expertise. Lack of external reputation evidence is
not automatically evidence of a bad reputation, especially for smaller creators.

Human quality raters evaluate Google's systems. Their ratings do not directly
change an individual page's ranking. Do not report an invented Google "E-E-A-T
score," Page Quality score, or Needs Met score.

## YMYL

Raise the bar for topics where inaccurate information can significantly harm
health, financial stability, safety, or societal welfare. Require authoritative,
traceable sources and appropriate qualified review for advice. Distinguish
personal lived experience from expert advice; the former can be valuable without
being sufficient for high-stakes factual recommendations.

If facts or qualified review are missing, flag the gap. Do not manufacture medical,
financial, legal, or civic authority to make a page look trustworthy.

## Who, how, and why

Make clear who is responsible for content, how it was produced where relevant,
and why it exists. Disclose methods, sponsorship, affiliate relationships, and
substantial automation when readers would reasonably expect that information.
Avoid presenting context-sensitive AI disclosure advice as a universal ranking rule.

Create for an intended audience that would benefit even without search traffic.
Avoid unsupported release dates, broad topic sprawl without expertise, arbitrary
word-count targets, synthetic freshness, and mass removal to "refresh" the site.

## Reviews, forums, and UGC

For reviews, require actual evidence of use or research, testing methodology,
quantitative measurements where available, advantages and limitations, relevant
alternatives, decision factors, and reasons for recommendations. Distinguish
first-hand tests from desk research. A ranked list should be useful on its own.
Qualify affiliate links and disclose incentives; never fabricate ratings or tests.

For forums and Q&A, assess the usefulness and context of the discussion, responsible
contributors, moderation, spam controls, and factual reliability. Personal accounts
can add authentic value. Do not assume a forum brand or a 2023 "Perspectives"
announcement guarantees current visibility.
